# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: using welcoming and
inclusive language, respecting differing opinions, gracefully accepting criticism, focusing
on what is best for the community, and showing empathy toward others. Examples of
unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting/derogatory
comments, public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Community leadership will consider and respond to reports, and may temporarily or permanently
remove contributors from the project for behavior they deem abusive, threatening, or
otherwise unacceptable. Leadership language and actions are bound by this Code of Conduct.

Instances may be reported to the maintainer via GitHub (open a private issue or use
security reporting for safety-adjacent matters). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
